Rules

Goal

Get a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over.

Card values

2–10 = face value; J/Q/K = 10; Ace = 1 or 11, whichever suits your hand better.

Hit or Stand

On your turn, take another card (hit) or keep your hand as is (stand).

Bust

If your hand goes over 21, you automatically lose, regardless of the dealer's hand.

Dealer rules

The dealer must keep hitting until reaching 17 or higher, then must stand.

Winning

You win if your hand is higher than the dealer's without busting, or if the dealer busts
